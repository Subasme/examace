📋 NEET Exam Guide

Smart strategies to maximise your NEET score

✅ Do's

1
Read every question and all 4 options completely before choosing — qualifying words like "always", "never", "most likely" change the answer
2
Attempt Biology first — it's the highest-scoring section (360 marks) with no complex calculations
3
Use the rough sheet for Physics numericals — write down known values and formula before solving
4
Mark uncertain questions and revisit — don't get stuck; move on and come back
5
Manage time: ~1 min per Biology Q, ~1.5 min Chemistry, ~2 min Physics — keep checking the clock
6
Trust NCERT — 85%+ of NEET questions come directly from NCERT text and diagrams

❌ Don'ts

1
Don't attempt a Physics or Chemistry question if completely unsure — negative marking (-1) will reduce your total
2
Don't spend more than 2 minutes on any single question — time loss compounds quickly
3
Don't change an answer you were initially confident about — first instinct is usually correct
4
Don't neglect NCERT diagrams and figures — labelled diagrams are frequently tested directly
5
Don't leave any Biology question unanswered — there is no negative mark risk if you make an educated guess
6
Don't panic over unfamiliar questions — eliminate impossible options and make a reasoned choice

💡 Study Tips

1
Revise NCERT Biology line by line — pay special attention to classification tables, genetics problems, and human physiology
2
For Physics, understand derivations and formulas conceptually — don't just memorise; practise numericals daily
3
For Chemistry, memorise named reactions, reagents, and exception compounds chapter by chapter
4
Use spaced repetition for revision: review chapters after 1 day, 1 week, and 1 month
5
Solve at least 5 previous NEET papers under timed conditions — it builds exam stamina and reveals weak areas
6
Focus revision on weak chapters — 20% of chapters contribute roughly 80% of marks
